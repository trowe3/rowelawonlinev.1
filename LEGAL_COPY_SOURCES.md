## Clarifications applied to the brief

- Divorce property division describes an equal-division presumption and exceptions rather than guaranteeing a 50/50 result. Marital-property classification and divorce division are related but distinct questions.
- Primary placement is described as where a child lives most of the time. The 25% / 92-day threshold is identified in its shared-placement support context, with equivalent care recognized.
- Minor-person guardianships generally use Chapter 48 procedures; a minor’s estate uses Chapter 54. Full, limited, temporary, and emergency categories are not applied indiscriminately to both.
- Adult guardianship depends on statutory findings and consideration of less restrictive alternatives, rather than diagnosis alone.
- An agreed GAL nomination still requires court appointment. Representation of a parent and service as GAL are separate roles.
- Adoption uses the requested two-step presentation while explaining that TPR is needed only where applicable and an existing spouse-parent’s rights remain in qualifying stepparent adoptions. Confirmatory LGBTQ+ adoption is described as an individually evaluated route, not universally available regardless of legal parentage or marital status.
- Virtual hearings are subject to court authorization; fully remote attendance is not guaranteed.
